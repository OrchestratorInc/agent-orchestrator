Allow an AO agent to use Chrome?

An Agent Orchestrator session wants to browse in this Chrome, with your sign-ins.

Session

If you allow it, the agent:

  • works only in its own AO tab group, never your other tabs;
  • can read and act on pages it opens there, signed in as you;
  • cannot read your cookies, history, bookmarks, or passwords;
  • stays off sites on the deny list (see Options).

You can stop every agent any time from the AO Browser Bridge toolbar button.
