## Actual-account results

The real desktop used three accounts added by the user. Two accounts produced overlapping terminal replies after a supported model was selected. Terminal and Chat handoffs preserved conversation history. Cancelling a pending switch preserved a queued Chat message. Temporarily disabling one account blocked that account while another continued; the account was re-enabled. Saved bindings and accounts survived a daemon restart with runner reattachment.

New live blockers remain:

1. Managed model validation consults the unrelated native catalog, and the installed client's default model is absent from the embedded catalog.
2. An exhausted account receives misleading authorization errors and loses visible usage despite a successful quota response.
3. A waiting switch becomes terminally failed across daemon restart and cannot be retried, although its source binding remains unchanged.
4. Account actions are clipped at the observed narrow window width. Keyboard activation verified removal confirmation; pointer usability did not pass.
5. Session account controls show IDs instead of the already-available email/label.

No real account was removed. Removal impact and exact confirmation were inspected without mutation. See [live results](parallel-session-delivery/user-flow/LIVE-RESULTS.md) for the bounded evidence and limitations.
